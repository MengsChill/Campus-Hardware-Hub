How a rental works

  1. Find & book

    Search by category, price and condition. Pick your dates and suggest when and where to meet.

  2. Pay into escrow

    The system holds the rental fee and deposit. The lender doesn't get paid until you have the item.

  3. Meet with a PIN

    Meet in person and check the item, then give the lender your 4-digit PIN.

  4. Return & get refunded

    Return it on time and your deposit comes back. If something breaks, an admin settles it fairly.

Trust Points

Everyone starts at 100. Returning on time and getting good reviews raises your score, and breaking the rules lowers it.

Fair disputes

Damage claims use photos and statements from both sides, and an admin decides what comes out of the deposit.

Honest reviews

Rate the person and the item after every rental, so the next student knows what to expect.
