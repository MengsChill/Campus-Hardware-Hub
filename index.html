How a rental works

  1. Find and book. Search by category, price and condition. Pick your dates and suggest when and where to meet.
  2. Pay into escrow. The system holds the rental fee and the deposit. The lender isn't paid until you have the item.
  3. Meet and check the item. When you're happy with it, give the lender your 4-digit PIN. That releases the rental fee.
  4. Return it. Bring it back on time and your deposit is refunded. If something gets damaged, an admin settles it from the deposit.

Keeping it fair

Trust Points
Everyone starts at 100. On-time returns and good reviews add points, and late returns or damage take them away. Accounts below 50 are reviewed by an admin.
Disputes
A lender can file a claim with photos, both sides add their statement, and an admin decides how much of the deposit goes to the lender.
Reviews
After each rental you rate the other person, and borrowers also rate the item, so the next student knows what to expect.
